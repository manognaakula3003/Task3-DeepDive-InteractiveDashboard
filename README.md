# Task 3 - Deep Dive Analysis & Interactive Dashboarding

## Objective

The objective of this project is to perform a deep-dive analysis on sales data and develop an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## Dataset

Sales Transaction Dataset

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Average Order Value
* Top Customer
* Top Product Line

## Analysis Performed

### Customer Performance Analysis

Identified the highest revenue-generating customers and evaluated customer contribution to total sales.

### Product Performance Analysis

Analyzed product categories and identified the most profitable product lines.

### Country-wise Performance Analysis

Examined revenue contribution across different countries and regions.

### Monthly Sales Trend Analysis

Studied seasonal sales patterns and identified peak-performing months.

### Deal Size Analysis

Evaluated the revenue contribution of small, medium, and large deals.

## Key Findings

* Euro Shopping Channel is the highest revenue-generating customer.
* Classic Cars is the best-performing product line.
* USA is the highest revenue-generating country.
* November recorded the highest sales.
* Medium-sized deals contribute the largest share of revenue.
